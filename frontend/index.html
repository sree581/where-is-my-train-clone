How Where Is My Train Works – Your Complete Railway Companion

Where is My Train is an online tool that lets you track your live train status without any complex process. This tracker shows you exactly where the train is on the map, how much distance it has covered, expected delays, and other platform numbers — all in one place. Our intelligent search system connects you to real-time data from Indian Railways, covering 12,000+ trains across 8,000+ railway stations.

Ever asked yourself, Where is my train right now? Whether you're planning a journey, tracking a loved one's travel, or waiting to board, knowing your train's exact location is crucial. That's where whereismytrain.live comes in — a simple yet powerful platform to check live train running status in real-time.
